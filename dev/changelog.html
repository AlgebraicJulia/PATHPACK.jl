Release Notes ​

Unreleased ​

The first public version of PATHPACK.jl. Install it from GitHub for now:

julia
using Pkg
Pkg.add(url = "https://github.com/AlgebraicJulia/PATHPACK.jl")

Solver ​

  • mstar, mlu and pstar: the star of a sparse matrix over a semiring, its factorization, and some of its rows, by a sparse direct method with a fill-reducing order from CliqueTrees.jl (AMF() by default).

  • lmul! and rmul! on a factorization: single-source and single-target queries without the whole star.

  • Semirings MinPlus, MaxPlus, MaxMin, MinMax, MinProd, MaxProd, PlusProd, OrAnd and AndOr, and the scalar interface for defining others.

  • Multithreaded on the CPU through the keyword nt.

GPUs ​

  • With CUDA.jl loaded: apsp_gpu and apsp_gpu! for all pairs on one NVIDIA GPU or several, apsp_plan to reuse the symbolic work, and sssp_gpu! for single sources.

Documentation ​

  • This site: a guide, 13 tutorials with downloadable notebooks, the benchmarks, and the library reference.
